Room Repeater

Pilot · Android Chrome · EN / DE / ES
How to use it. Press Test sound in Settings first, then allow the microphone when Chrome asks (it needs internet). Keep the phone close to her, somewhere quiet. After she taps a picture and hears the word, stay silent while the ring is green — the app can’t tell your voice from hers. If she doesn’t say it, just carry on; it comes back later. It’s built for one session a day.
